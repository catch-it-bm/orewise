Questions

What counts as a document?

One file is one document, whether it's a one-page memo or a 300-page manual. Each plan also has a storage limit.

Which file types are supported?

PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, plain text, Markdown, HTML and more. Support for scanned documents (OCR) is on the roadmap.

Where can my documents come from?

Direct upload and Dropbox at launch, plus shared network drives for self-hosted installations. SharePoint/OneDrive, Google Drive, Confluence and Notion are coming next.

What happens when I reach my document limit?

Nothing breaks. Existing documents keep working; new files simply wait until you free up space or upgrade, and they're picked up automatically.

Can the website widget leak internal documents?

No. A public widget can only ever search collections you've explicitly marked as public. This is enforced on our servers, not in the browser.

Does Orewise make things up?

Orewise is instructed to answer only from your documents and to say when it doesn't know. Every answer links to its sources so you can check.
